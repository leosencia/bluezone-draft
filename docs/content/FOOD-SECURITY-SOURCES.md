## Aeroponics research

+114% irrigation water-use efficiency: Carotti et al. (2023), Agricultural
Water Management 285, 108365. A University of Bologna lettuce experiment
compared high-pressure aeroponics with ebb-and-flow substrate culture.
The irrigation-only measure was 69.7 versus 32.5 grams fresh weight per litre.
The paper reports a 114% increase in this ratio. This is NOT a 114% water saving,
nor an all-farm water metric; climate-control and management water are excluded.

- [Publication / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.agwat.2023.108365)
- [University repository](https://cris.unibo.it/bitstream/11585/933655/1/Carotti%20et%20al%202023.pdf)

The figure is explicitly labelled as a published study result, not measured
Bluezone performance, immediately beside the statistic. No generic ROI,
growth-rate, pesticide-free or certified-sustainability claims are added.

## Bluezone's potential contribution

Local crop production, recovery of unused nutrient solution, and shorter
routes are framed as potential contributions rather than quantified impacts.
The page acknowledges crop suitability, access, affordability, power and
operating requirements. It does not suggest that microgreens replace staple
agriculture or alone resolve hunger.

The offerings section retains 70 square metres of canopy and five tiers from
CAT p.4 (see BLUEZONE-CONTENT-SOURCES.md). These are proposed design figures,
not validated yields. Existing concept imagery is reused, with descriptive
alternative text and visible captions for the root-zone, produce and BioCube
visuals. Removed homepage sections remain in source for reference.
